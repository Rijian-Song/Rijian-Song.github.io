Rijian Song

Postdoctoral Researcher at University College Dublin · CTO at Blafar Ltd.

I build injectable hydrogels for soft-tissue repair. My long-term mission is to create the soft interface where humans, machines, and living systems can work together—connecting biomaterials with biology today, and eventually with artificial intelligence.
