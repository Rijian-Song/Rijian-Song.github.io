University College Dublin Biomaterials · Injectable Hydrogels · Translation

My work focuses on mechanically robust and clinically practical hydrogel systems for biomedical applications. A central theme is to improve the performance of injectable and adhesive hydrogels without losing the features that make them attractive for translation: mild processing, biological compatibility, handling convenience, and adaptability to real tissue environments.

Research Focus

Hydrogel materials for demanding biological interfaces

Hyaluronic Acid Hydrogels

Design of HA-based and polysaccharide hydrogel networks with tunable mechanics, degradation, injectability, and tissue-relevant performance.

Wet Tissue Adhesion

Cohesion-adhesion balanced hydrogel adhesives for wet, dynamic, and biologically complex tissue surfaces.

Chronic Wound Repair

Injectable and self-healing wound dressing systems for antibacterial treatment, exosome delivery, and chronic wound repair.
