Rijian Song

Postdoctoral Researcher at University College Dublin · CTO at Blafar Ltd.

Biomaterials · Hydrogels · Translation

Dublin, Ireland

My work sits at the interface of polymer chemistry, biomedical engineering, and translation. I develop injectable and adhesive hydrogel technologies that connect molecular design with tissue repair, practical handling, quality systems, and real-world use.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at University College Dublin and Chief Technology Officer at Blafar Ltd.. My research focuses on hydrogel structural design, wet-tissue adhesion, chronic wound repair, and translational biomaterials.

The research overview describes the three connected systems behind this work. The publications page is populated from my public Google Scholar record, with the latest publications added as they become available.
