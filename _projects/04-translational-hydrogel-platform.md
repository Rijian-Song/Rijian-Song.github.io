An injectable hydrogel can support different application directions only when the formulation and evidence package are designed for the intended use. This platform view connects molecular cargo, living cargo, soft-tissue volume, and in situ scaffold formation with the corresponding performance, safety, reproducibility, quality, and regulatory questions.

These are design and translational directions, not one-formulation-fits-all claims.
