This research direction focuses on the relationship between network architecture, injectability, toughness, and recovery. The goal is to design materials that can be handled and delivered practically while retaining useful mechanical performance after placement.

Key design variables include dynamic bonding, framework–chain integration, water content, network homogeneity, and the balance between flow and recovery.
