Wet adhesion is an interface problem and a bulk-material problem at the same time. This work studies how cohesive strength, interfacial interactions, and handling conditions can be tuned together rather than optimised in isolation.

The intended design space includes tissue sealing, soft-tissue repair, and dynamic interfaces where water, motion, and biological complexity challenge conventional adhesives.
