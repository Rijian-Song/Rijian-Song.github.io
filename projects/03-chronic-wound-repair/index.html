chronic wound repair

Injectable and self-healing systems for difficult-to-heal tissue environments.

This direction brings together hydrogel mechanics, antibacterial or therapeutic functions, and the microenvironment of chronic wounds. The emphasis is on formulation-specific evidence: local delivery, tissue contact, degradation, handling, and biological response must be evaluated together.
