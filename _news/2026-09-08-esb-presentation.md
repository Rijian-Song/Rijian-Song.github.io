I presented our work, _“Injectable hyaluronic acid hydrogel-enabled mesenchymal stem cell therapy for diabetic chronic wound repair,”_ at the **35th Annual Conference of the European Society for Biomaterials (ESB 2026)** in Antwerp, Belgium.

The talk was part of the **RSC Biomaterials Chemistry Interest Group Symposium: Injectable Hydrogels for TERM Applications**. I discussed our approach to using injectable hyaluronic acid hydrogels to support MSC survival, spheroid formation and paracrine activity for diabetic wound repair.

Thank you to our collaborators and colleagues in Professor Wenxin Wang’s research group at University College Dublin for their contributions to this work.
